####Who am I?

I'm a PhD candidate in Urban Land Economics at UBC's Sauder School of Business. I became interested in Economics as a high school student because it combined my interests in politics and studying how people work, and I was fortunate enough to have several BYU economics professors live in my neighbourhood and serve as wonderful mentors. Later I spent two years in Paraguay where I witnessed extreme poverty but also strong community and happy people. Deciding I wanted to help improve public policy, I moved to DC and worked as a Research Assistant in the Real Estate Finance section of the Federal Reserve Board. Studying the housing market and experiencing the differences between my hometown of Provo, Utah with Paraguay and Washington, DC led me to embrace housing and urban policy as my area of focus. In my spare time you can find me running in Pacific Spirit forest or playing with my 4-year-old son.
