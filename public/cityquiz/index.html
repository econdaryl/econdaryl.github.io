A LITTLE LOCAL KNOWLEDGE

Canada, by heart.

Type a city or town and press Enter. Each correct answer colours its area on the map. Darker colours mean more people, on a logarithmic population scale. Your progress is saved in this browser.

These areas follow concentrations of people rather than municipal borders. Nearby towns may share one area: naming any of its recorded settlements counts, but that area can only be scored once.

Accents, capital letters and common Saint/St. spellings are flexible. Exact city names take priority over secondary names; the largest matching area wins. Add a province, like “Stirling, AB” or “Stirling Alberta”, to choose a specific place. A typed province overrides the dropdown.

Use “Finish & reveal” to see the remaining answers. Your score will still reflect only the places you found yourself.

 BEHIND THE ATLAS

Places, not city limits.

The map uses 2025 GHSL projected population, smoothed at 4 km. Small catchments merge only through shared borders. Each playable area is contiguous and has at least 1,000 people; isolated smaller islands are excluded.

Names and accepted guesses come from GeoNames settlement records. Where settlement population is unknown, a candidate name is used. These names are a first pass and may need correction.

National and provincial coverage count only playable areas. Cross-border areas count entirely toward their main province. The muted terrain appears only outside playable areas. Polygons are simplified for display; population totals come from the unsimplified analysis. Colours show each original component’s population before merging; listed populations and scores use the combined total. Population figures are model estimates, not official municipal census counts.
